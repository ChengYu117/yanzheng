## 冻结解释（英文原文）

- **Short name:** direct what-question
- **Surface/linguistic hypothesis:** The feature tracks main-clause direct interrogatives headed by what or what's, often after discourse markers like okay, so, or and.
- **Behavioral/discourse hypothesis:** The feature may mark counselor questions that invite the client to explore their own perspective, meaning, options, or anticipated consequences rather than presenting a summary or directive.
- **Primary explanation:** The most stable distinction is linguistic: Group A uses what as the head of a direct question, while Group B frequently uses what in embedded or non-question environments, with only partial boundary overlap.
